What Resonance does

Keep audio controls together.

Use the small media island for quick changes, then open the full console for everything else.

Playback

Play, pause, skip, seek, and switch between the players your session exposes.

Lyrics

Show lyrics beside the current track when the player provides them.

Audio routing

See available outputs and send sound where it belongs.

Visual feedback

Use the spectrum and waveform to make changes easy to follow.

Start here

Open the audio console.

Start playback in your normal player, then open Resonance from the media island or launcher.

$ blackhole open media
$ blackhole open audio
