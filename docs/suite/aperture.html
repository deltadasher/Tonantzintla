What Aperture does

Build the bar around your day.

Keep the surface small and useful. Give every island one clear job and one clear way to open its full tool.

Bar editor

Move islands, set their position, pick a layout, and change the bar’s appearance in one place.

System readings

See CPU, memory, GPU, disk, volume, network, and device status at a glance.

Two monitors

Keep the arrangement aware of each output so a click opens on the monitor you are using.

Small surfaces

Let the bar stay quiet until you ask an island to open something larger.
